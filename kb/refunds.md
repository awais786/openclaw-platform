# Refund policy

We offer a full refund within **14 days** of purchase, no questions asked.
After 14 days, refunds are handled case by case by the billing team.

Subscriptions can be cancelled any time from **Settings → Billing**; cancelling stops
future charges but does not refund the current billing period unless you are within the
14-day window. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
